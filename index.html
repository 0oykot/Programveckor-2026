About us!

Stay on top of your assignments with a clear and intuitive structure designed to make planning and organizing your work easy. Keep all your tasks, deadlines, and priorities in one central place, so you always know what needs to be done and when. Break larger assignments into smaller, manageable steps and track your progress as you go, reducing stress and helping you stay focused. To keep you motivated, the built-in point system rewards your effort and consistency, turning productivity into a positive and engaging experience. Whether you’re managing daily tasks or long-term projects, this website helps you stay organized, motivated, and in control of your workload.

Start your journey!

Why us?

  • Clear structure to plan and track your work
  • Organize all your assignments in one place
  • Point system that rewards progress and boosts motivation
  • Stay focused and on top of deadlines
  • Turn productivity into a habit

We are a group of five students — Viggo, Suzan, Johayna, Valter, and Simon — who came together with a shared goal of making student life more organized and motivating. Through our own experiences with assignments, deadlines, and procrastination, we saw the need for a simple tool that helps bring structure and encouragement into everyday work. By combining our different ideas, skills, and perspectives, we created this website to help students stay on track, stay motivated, and feel more in control of their workload.
